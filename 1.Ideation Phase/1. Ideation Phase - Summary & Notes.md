# Phase 1: Ideation Phase
**Project:** Auto Ticket Classification using Flow Designer  
**Scope:** ServiceNow Global Application Scope  
**Team ID:** AGY-SNOW-2025  

---

## 1. Phase Overview
The Ideation Phase establishes the strategic foundation of the project. It identifies chronic operational bottlenecks in academic IT support environments—specifically the manual categorization of incoming incident requests—and applies design thinking frameworks to formulate an automated, human-centered solution.

## 2. Key Deliverables in this Folder
1. `Define Problem Statements Template.docx`: Customer Problem Statement table articulating challenges from the perspectives of IT Agents, Students, Teachers, and IT Leadership using the 5-Whys methodology.
2. `Empathy Map Canvas.docx`: In-depth analysis of what Academic Callers and Support Technicians Say, Do, Think, Feel, Hear, and See.
3. `Brainstorming- Idea Generation- Prioritizaation Template.docx`: Comprehensive evaluation of candidate architectures (Manual Dispatch, Scripted Business Rules, Machine Learning, Flow Designer) and justification for selecting ServiceNow Flow Designer.

## 3. High-Level Problem Statements Summary
| Persona | Customer Need | Core Obstacle | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IT Helpdesk Agent** | Triage and route tickets quickly | 40% of time spent manually selecting Category/Subcategory | Overwhelmed during morning surges; delayed routing |
| **Teacher / Faculty** | Uninterrupted classroom tech (Wi-Fi, Projectors) | Tickets sit unassigned and unacknowledged for hours | Classroom instruction stalled; lost academic time |
| **Student** | Access learning portals & submit coursework | No visibility into whether IT received password/login ticket | Anxiety over deadlines; duplicated ticket submissions |
| **IT Management** | Maintain strict SLAs and clean ITSM analytics | High rate of human misclassification & category errors | Skewed operational metrics; audit compliance risks |

## 4. Key Design Takeaways
- **Minimize End-User Friction:** The ticket submission form should only require **Caller** and **Short Description**.
- **Deterministic Auto-Classification:** Common keywords (Wi-Fi, Projector, Password, Slow Computer) must automatically assign Category & Subcategory.
- **Relational Integrity:** Subcategories must be dynamically filtered based on Category using ServiceNow Dictionary Dependencies.
- **Instant Assurance:** An automated confirmation email must be dispatched to the caller immediately upon ticket creation.
